# Industries That Use HRMS (by Category)

Priority key: **High** = strong, recurring need and proven buying behavior in the Philippines. **Medium** = real need, but harder to sell or more specialized. **Niche** = valuable only if you build for its specific rules.

---

## 1. Government and Public Sector

| Segment | Key HRMS needs | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| LGUs (municipalities, cities, provinces) | Plantilla, salary grades, PERA/allowances, GSIS, CSC leave rules, service records, COA-ready documents | High (fits your civic-tech background) |
| Barangays | Officials and staff honoraria, simple DTR, appointment records | Medium |
| National agencies, SUCs, GOCCs | Plantilla, performance (SPMS), training, DBM and CSC compliance | Medium |
| Public schools and DepEd units | Teacher records, leave, service credits | Niche |

## 2. BPO, IT and Services

| Segment | Key HRMS needs | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| BPO / call centers | Shift scheduling, night differential, overtime, high attrition tracking, mass hiring, attendance integration | High |
| IT and software companies | Remote and hybrid attendance, performance, leave, equity or bonus tracking | Medium |
| Staffing and manpower agencies | Many clients per employer, deployment tracking, per-client payroll, contract expiry | High |
| Freelancer or contractor platforms | Contractor onboarding, invoicing, tax withholding | Niche |

## 3. Retail, Hospitality and Food

| Segment | Key HRMS needs | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Retail chains, malls, supermarkets | Multi-branch, shift rosters, seasonal hires, holiday pay | High |
| Restaurants and fast food | High turnover, part-time scheduling, tip or service charge distribution | High |
| Hotels and resorts | Rotating shifts, seasonal staffing, service charge distribution | Medium |
| Convenience and franchise networks | Branch-level HR with central payroll | Medium |

## 4. Manufacturing and Industrial

| Segment | Key HRMS needs | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Factories and plants | Biometric attendance, shifts, overtime, safety and training records | High |
| Construction firms | Project-based workforce, daily-rate workers, site attendance, safety certifications | Medium |
| Agriculture and plantations | Seasonal and piece-rate workers, remote sites, cash payroll | Niche |
| Mining, energy, utilities | Certifications, hazard pay, remote deployment | Niche |

## 5. Healthcare

| Segment | Key HRMS needs | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Hospitals | 24/7 rosters, PRC license and credential expiry, on-call pay, many job classes | High |
| Clinics, diagnostic centers | Smaller rosters, license tracking | Medium |

## 6. Education

|---|---|---|

## 9. Non-profit and Social Sector

| Segment | Key HRMS needs | Priority |
|---|---|---|

## 10. Small Business and Startups

| Segment | Key HRMS needs | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| SMEs, 10 to 200 employees | Affordable all-in-one: records, DTR, leave, payroll, government remittances | High |
| Startups | Fast onboarding, remote work, equity tracking | Medium |

---

## Company Size Tiers

| Tier | Typical need | Buying behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Micro (under 20) | Payroll and DTR, price sensitive | Often use spreadsheets; hard to convert |
| Small (20 to 200) | Records, attendance, leave, payroll | Best volume segment |
| Mid-size (200 to 1,000) | Workflows, approvals, reporting, integrations | Longer sales, higher value |
| Enterprise (1,000+) | Customization, SSO, multi-entity, audit | Competes with global vendors |

---

## Recommended Starting Segments (my assessment)

1. **LGUs**: closest to your existing work and relationships, with specific compliance needs that generic vendors handle poorly. Procurement is slow and rules-heavy, so account for that.
2. **SMEs in retail, manufacturing or services (20 to 200 staff)**: largest market, but the most crowded with existing products. You need a clear reason to be chosen.
3. **Security and manpower agencies**: strong, specific pain (deployment, rotating shifts, client-based payroll) and fewer polished local options.

**Pushback:** pick one segment, not several. Each industry has different payroll rules and attendance patterns, and a product that tries to serve all of them early usually serves none well. Validate demand by interviewing HR staff in your chosen segment before you build.

Market and competitor claims here are from general knowledge; verify current vendors and demand in your target segment before committing.
